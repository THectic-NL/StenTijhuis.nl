Privacy

Last updated: 2 August 2026

Who runs this site?

Sten Tijhuis. This domain is used for a personal portfolio and for personal email.

Purpose

This site exists mainly as a digital business card; something to link to in emails and share on social media. That's it. No data is collected.

Cookies & trackers

None. This site is completely cookie-free and tracker-free.

Data & logging

Nothing is collected or stored. Your visit leaves no trace.

Bot & scraper protection

Some content on this site is technically obfuscated to deter automated scraping and AI training crawlers, using the ShieldFont technique (covered by The Register). It's a presentation-layer technique: custom fonts render correctly for humans, but anything reading raw markup gets decoy text. It does not identify, track, or collect any information about visitors, human or automated. It has no relation to the "no data is collected" statement above; that remains fully true.

External links

Links to LinkedIn, GitHub, GitLab and others take you off this site. Those services have their own policies.
